Settings

  1. Add the Get homework button. On a computer, show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows, Cmd+Shift+B on Mac), then drag this button onto it:

    Can't drag? Copy the text below, make a new bookmark named Get homework, and paste it as the address (URL).
